## About
{: #about }

I am currently an Assistant Professor in the [School of Electronic Information and Communications (EIC)](https://eic.hust.edu.cn/) at [Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)](https://english.hust.edu.cn/), China. I was previously a Boya Postdoctoral Fellow at the [State Key Laboratory of General Artificial Intelligence](https://sai.pku.edu.cn/znxyenglish/info/1372/2592.htm), [School of Intelligence Science and Technology](https://sai.pku.edu.cn/znxyenglish/), [Peking University](https://english.pku.edu.cn/), hosted by Prof. [Zhouchen Lin](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=TanjFwoAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN). I obtained my Ph.D. from the [School of Electronic Information and Electrical Engineering (SEIEE)](https://english.seiee.sjtu.edu.cn/english/index.htm), [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/), under the supervision of Prof. [Robert Caiming Qiu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=FTLNXX8AAAAJ&hl=zh-CN). I received my undergraduate degree from the [Department of Mathematics](https://njunju.nju.edu.cn/EN/7f/77/c7136a163703/page.htm) at [Nanjing University](https://www.nju.edu.cn/en/).

My research primarily focuses on the theoretical and computational aspects of machine learning. I am particularly interested in implicit neural networks, iterative neural computation, and efficient inference, as well as their connections to optimization, dynamical systems, high-dimensional statistics, and random matrix theory. Recent topics include deep equilibrium models, neural ODEs/SDEs, looped models, and test-time computation. I also explore machine learning applications in wireless communication and signal processing.
